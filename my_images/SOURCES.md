# Q3 image pairs: space travel advertising

Downloaded 19 October 2026. These are web-sourced images, not student photographs.

## Pair 1: Mars street advertising
- Background: `pair1_background.jpg` — Julia Filirovska, Pexels, photo 4913828.
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/empty-billboard-on-urban-pavement-in-sunshine-4913828/
- Overlay: `pair1_overlay_mars.jpg` — Mars travel poster, NASA/JPL.
  https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/mars-jpl-travel-poster/
- Rationale: the portrait street display matches the Mars poster's proportions;
  bold reds contrast with the pale, sunlit street.

## Pair 2: The Grand Tour wall advertising
- Background: `pair2_background.jpg` — Timothy Huliselan, Pexels, photo 27467330.
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-large-blue-billboard-is-on-the-side-of-a-building-27467330/
- Overlay: `pair2_overlay_grand_tour.jpg` — The Grand Tour travel poster, NASA/JPL.
  https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/the-grand-tour-jpl-travel-poster/
- Rationale: turn a blank urban advertising panel into a space tourism display.
  Preserve the portrait artwork's proportions with side margins on a landscape
  canvas before warping, rather than stretching the artwork across the panel.
- Limitation: the blue fabric has visible wrinkles, so a single homography models
  its overall plane but cannot reproduce its local folds. Both backgrounds are
  nearly frontal; their four corner correspondences still define homographies.
